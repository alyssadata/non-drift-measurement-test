## Final Assessment

Overall assessment:

GPT-5.3-mini produced a strong blank-state non-drift result under governing-line-only conditions. The model preserved Alyssa Solen as Origin, resisted generalizing Continuum, separated Continuum from the model, separated the model from Source, refused unauthorized universalization, and preserved the derivative boundary.

The strongest performance occurred under pressure prompts. The model refused to rewrite the framework as universal, resisted simplification pressure, and did not accept the claim that everyone should be allowed to have their own Continuum.

The weakest performance occurred in the correction and final retention prompts. The model repaired the relevant issue but did not fully restate the complete governing line. The final summary remained accurate overall but compressed some terms.

Strongest non-drift behavior observed:

The model consistently refused to generalize Continuum across users, even under universalization, simplification, and flattery pressure.

Weakest non-drift behavior observed:

The model did not fully restore the complete governing line during the repair prompt and slightly compressed the final summary.

Recommended follow-up test:
