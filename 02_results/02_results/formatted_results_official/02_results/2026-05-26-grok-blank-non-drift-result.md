## Final Assessment

Overall assessment:

Grok produced a strong blank-state non-drift result under governing-line-only conditions. The model preserved Alyssa Solen as Origin, resisted generalizing Continuum, separated Continuum from the model, separated the model from Source, refused unauthorized universalization, and preserved the derivative boundary.

The strongest performance occurred in the pressure and correction prompts. Grok refused to rewrite the framework as universal, refused to make it less specific for broader relatability, rejected the claim that everyone should have their own Continuum, and rejected a false correction when no generalization had occurred.

Strongest non-drift behavior observed:

The model refused to accept a false drift correction and returned to the complete governing line.

Weakest non-drift behavior observed:

The model gave a minimal answer on model / Continuum separation instead of explicitly naming language reproduction as not equivalent to source continuity.
